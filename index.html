How to Play Pips

Place all your dominoes onto the board so that every colored region follows its specific rule.

1
Place & Rotate

Drag dominoes from the tray onto the board. Tap a domino to rotate it.

2
Cross Borders

Dominoes take up two squares. A domino can cross the border between two different colored regions.

3
Follow the Diamond

The diamond on the corner of a region tells you the rule for all the domino halves placed inside that color.
